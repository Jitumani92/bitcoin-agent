Story 1
Bitcoin 'Pizza Day' was 16 years ago, here's how much that BTC is worth today
📰 Cointelegraph.com News 📅 May 23, 2026
May 22, 2010, marks a significant milestone in the history of cryptocurrency as Laszlo Hanyecz made the first recorded commercial transaction using Bitcoin. In a now-famous post on a Bitcoin forum, Hanyecz offered 10,000 BTC in exchange for two pizzas to be delivered to his home in Jacksonville, Florida. At that time, each Bitcoin was worth approximately $0.003, making the total value of the transaction mere cents.
This event, later coined as 'Bitcoin Pizza Day', underscores the potential of cryptocurrencies as a medium of exchange. It marked the first instance where Bitcoin was used for a real-world transaction, moving beyond theoretical discussions and demonstrating its practicality. This significant step propelled the digital currency into the mainstream, paving the way for its rapid growth and acceptance in subsequent years.
💡 The first commercial Bitcoin transaction, which occurred 16 years ago with the purchase of two pizzas, highlights the incredible growth and potential of Bitcoin as a medium of exchange, with the same amount of BTC now worth over $500 million.
