Bitcoin ‘golden cross’ appears for the first time since 2023: Will BTC price rally?
📰 Cointelegraph.com News 📅 May 12, 2026
In a significant development, the cryptocurrency market has witnessed a noteworthy event. For the first time since 2023, Bitcoin's MVRV (Market Value to Realized Value) ratio has displayed a 'golden cross,' indicating a potential shift towards bullish momentum for the world's leading digital currency. This rare occurrence suggests that BTC could be entering a new phase of growth, as the market structure strengthens around it.
The MVRV ratio is an important technical indicator used to measure Bitcoin's performance against its realized value (the average price at which coins were last moved). When the short-term MVRV crosses above the long-term MVRV, we have what's known as a 'golden cross.' Historically, this event has often preceded substantial price increases in BTC. However, it is essential to note that while this cross is an encouraging sign, it does not guarantee future price movements.
💡 The appearance of a Bitcoin 'golden cross' suggests a shift towards bullish momentum for the world's leading digital currency, which may signal the start of a new bull market and offer potential opportunities for investors, but it is essential to approach any investment in BTC with caution due to the market's volatility.
