CBOE weighs converting BTC, ETH continuous futures into perpetual futures: Report
📰 Cointelegraph.com News 📅 June 24, 2026
In a potential shakeup for the cryptocurrency market, global markets trading firm CBOE Global Markets Inc. is reportedly considering transforming its Bitcoin (BTC) and Ethereum (ETH) continuous futures into perpetual contracts. This move comes as regulatory changes in the US accelerate the adoption of crypto perpetual futures, with rivals Coinbase and Kalshi expanding their offerings in this area.
The shift to perpetual futures could significantly impact Bitcoin by offering more flexible trading options for investors. Unlike traditional futures contracts, which expire on a specific date, perpetual futures do not have an expiration date, allowing for uninterrupted trading and potential increased liquidity. This change could attract a broader range of participants to the market, potentially boosting Bitcoin's price volatility and overall adoption.
💡 CBOE's potential shift towards perpetual futures for Bitcoin and Ethereum could open up new trading opportunities for investors while potentially boosting the cryptocurrency market's liquidity and volatility. However, increased leverage risks are a concern that investors should be aware of.

Story 6
Bitcoin funding rate hits 2-week high: Is $70K next?
📰 Cointelegraph.com News 📅 June 24, 2026
The funding rate for Bitcoin, a fee that traders pay to each other when they hold long or short positions in perpetual swap contracts, has reached its highest level in two weeks. This indicates growing optimism among investors as the rate represents a premium or discount paid daily by long and short positions respectively to ensure parity between spot and derivatives markets.
The surge in the funding rate is significant for Bitcoin because it mirrors increased investor appetite for BTC. A positive funding rate, as observed currently, typically occurs during bullish market phases when long positions are favored over short ones, reflecting a general belief that the price of Bitcoin will rise.
