Boltz Launches Non-Custodial USDC Swaps, Bridging Bitcoin Directly to Circle’s Regulated Dollar
📰 Bitcoin Magazine 📅 May 08, 2026
In a significant move for cross-border transactions and Bitcoin adoption, Boltz has introduced trustless Bitcoin-to-USDC swaps using Circle's CCTP (Centralized Compliant Transfer Protocol). This innovation enables the instant movement between Bitcoin layers including Lightning, and the regulated stablecoin, USDC, accepted by major players like Stripe, Coinbase, Visa, and global institutions. The groundbreaking feature eliminates the need for accounts or custody (Boltz, 2023).
The development signifies a crucial step forward in Bitcoin's integration into traditional financial systems. By offering direct access to Circle's regulated USDC, Boltz is addressing one of the main obstacles to broader Bitcoin adoption: the lack of regulatory compliance and the perceived risk associated with it (Bitcoin Magazine, 2023).
💡 Boltz's non-custodial USDC swaps bridge Bitcoin directly to Circle's regulated dollar, making it easier for investors to diversify and conduct cross-border transactions with a stable digital asset.
