Why is Bitcoin falling despite pro-crypto Kevin Warsh becoming Fed chair?
📰 Cointelegraph.com News 📅 May 25, 2026
In an unexpected turn of events, the value of Bitcoin has been on a downward spiral since the announcement of Kevin Warsh's appointment as the new Federal Reserve chairman. Although Warsh is known for his pro-crypto stance, rising short-term bond yields and his past hawkish comments are reviving fears of a potential December interest rate hike. This prospect is causing jitters in the cryptocurrency market, as higher rates often lead to a stronger U.S. dollar, which could slam the brakes on Bitcoin's recovery.
The impact of this development on Bitcoin is significant, as the world's leading digital currency has shown sensitivity to monetary policy changes in the past. The concern among investors is that a rate hike would lead to increased borrowing costs and potentially slow down economic growth, thereby decreasing demand for riskier assets like Bitcoin. This could further drive down Bitcoin's value, which has already lost over 50% of its peak worth this year.
💡 The appointment of Kevin Warsh as the new Federal Reserve chairman has led to a fall in Bitcoin's value due to fears of a potential December interest rate hike, which could impact the digital currency's recovery in the short term. Investors need to closely monitor Warsh's monetary policies for both challenges and opportunities.

SEC approves Nasdaq to list Bitcoin index options on the exchange
📰 Cointelegraph.com News 📅 May 25, 2026
In a significant move for the cryptocurrency market, the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) has given its approval for Nasdaq to list Bitcoin index options on its platform. This decision marks a major step towards mainstream acceptance of Bitcoin as an investment asset. The cash-settled, European-style contracts will trade under the ticker QBTC on Phlx, a FINRA-regulated subsidiary of Nasdaq.
The approval is crucial for Bitcoin as it opens up new avenues for investors to gain exposure to the cryptocurrency without directly buying or holding it. This move is expected to increase liquidity and reduce volatility in the Bitcoin market, making it more attractive for institutional investors. The decision also underscores the growing recognition of Bitcoin as a legitimate investment asset by traditional financial institutions.
💡 The SEC's approval for Nasdaq to list Bitcoin index options is a significant step towards mainstream acceptance of Bitcoin as an investment asset and could increase liquidity and reduce volatility in the market.

$1.26B Bitcoin ETF outflows spark ‘contrarian’ buy signal: Santiment
📰 Cointelegraph.com News 📅 May 25, 2026
In a recent development, Bitcoin exchange-traded funds (ETFs) have seen significant outflows totaling approximately $1.26 billion. This trend has been observed over the past week, causing some analysts to view it as a potential 'contrarian' buying opportunity for investors. According to Santiment, an on-chain analytics platform, such outflows have historically correlated with conditions favorable for patient accumulation rather than panic.
The reason this matters for Bitcoin is that ETF outflows can often signal investor hesitation or fear, which could lead to a drop in the cryptocurrency's price. However, Santiment's analysis suggests that these outflows might instead indicate a buying opportunity, as they could signal a shift in sentiment from FOMO (Fear of Missing Out) to a more strategic approach among investors.
💡 The ongoing Bitcoin ETF outflows, according to Santiment, might indicate a buying opportunity due to historical correlations with conditions favorable for patient accumulation.
