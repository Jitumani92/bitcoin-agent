Story 6
Seven major Bitcoin mining pools join Stratum V2, working group
📰 Cointelegraph.com News 📅 May 11, 2026
Seven of the leading Bitcoin mining pools have announced their collaboration to adopt Stratum Version 2 (Stratum V2) and participate in a working group aimed at improving the efficiency and transparency of the Bitcoin mining process. The participating pools include F2Pool, Poolin, Antpool, Viabtc, Slush Pool, BTC.com, and Binance Pool.
This collaboration matters for Bitcoin because it aims to enhance the decentralization and security of the network by making the mining process more democratic. Individual miners in a mining pool must rely on block templates provided by the pool operator, which can potentially introduce centralization risks if a single pool has too much control over template creation. By adopting Stratum V2, these major pools are committing to greater transparency and fairness in their operations.
💡 Seven major Bitcoin mining pools joining Stratum V2 and a working group highlights their commitment to improving the network's efficiency, transparency, and decentralization, which could boost investor confidence in the cryptocurrency's future growth potential.
