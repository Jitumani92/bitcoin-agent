Bitcoin just $5K away from ‘best investment opportunity’ of bear market

📰 Cointelegraph.com News 📅 July 01, 2026 Original Source →

In a surprising turn, Bitcoin has seen a recent selloff that has brought it within striking distance of its realized price - a significant milestone in previous bear markets. The realized price is the average cost basis of all Bitcoin in circulation, and when Bitcoin reaches this level, it often signals a bottoming zone for the cryptocurrency. This means that the current market value of Bitcoin is lower than the average price at which investors purchased their Bitcoins.

This development matters because it indicates that Bitcoin's price may have overshot its intrinsic value during the bull run and is now correcting towards a more stable level. Historically, when Bitcoin reached this point in previous bear markets, it provided a strong buying opportunity for investors who were able to purchase Bitcoin at a discount.

If Bitcoin continues to drop and reaches its realized price of around $30,000, it would represent an excellent investment opportunity for those who believe in the long-term potential of cryptocurrency. While there are no guarantees in investing, given the past trends, this could be a prime time for investors to consider buying Bitcoin at a lower price point.

💡 Key Takeaway

The current Bitcoin selloff has brought it within 10% of its realized price - a level that historically marks a bottoming zone in bear markets and may signal an excellent investment opportunity for those who believe in the long-term potential of cryptocurrency.
